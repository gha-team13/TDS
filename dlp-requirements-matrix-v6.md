# Enterprise DLP Requirements Matrix & Architecture Specifications (v5)

This document synthesises the business and functional requirements extracted from the enterprise Data Loss Prevention (DLP) architecture JAD sessions and requirements-gathering transcripts. This updated version (v5) incorporates **Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)**, **GenAI prompt redaction business and functional requirements**, **humanised, real-world examples**, an **enterprise technical friction & deconfliction matrix**, and a comprehensive **DLP Glossary of Terms and Acronyms**.
